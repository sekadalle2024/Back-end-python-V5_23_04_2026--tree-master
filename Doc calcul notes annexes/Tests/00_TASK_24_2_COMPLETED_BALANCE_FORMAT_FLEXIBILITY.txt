Property 21: Balance Format Flexibility

For any balance file with column name variations (multiple spaces, different 
separators), the Balance_Parser must automatically detect and normalize column 
names, and must accept both comma and period as decimal separators, and must 
accept formats of numbers with or without thousand separators.
