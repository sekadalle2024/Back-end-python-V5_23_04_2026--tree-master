================================================================================
SUPPORTED FORMAT VARIATIONS
================================================================================

Column Name Variations:
  - Accents: "Numéro" ↔ "Numero", "Débit" ↔ "Debit"
  - Spaces: "Ant Débit" ↔ "Ant  Débit" ↔ "Ant   Débit"
  - Separators: "Ant Débit" ↔ "Ant_Debit" ↔ "Ant-Debit"
  - Abbreviations: "Solde D" ↔ "Solde Débit"

Decimal Separators:
  - Period: "1000000.00"
  - Comma: "1000000,00"

Thousand Separators:
  - Space: "1 000 000"
  - Comma: "1,000,000"
  - Period: "1.000.000"
  - None: "1000000"

Combined Examples:
  - "1 000 000,00" (space thousand, comma decimal)
  - "1,000,000.00" (comma thousand, period decimal)
  - "1.000.000,00" (period thousand, comma decimal)
